# AI as Modern Alchemy

**A research program.**

The alchemists tried to transmute lead into gold. We're transmuting computation into cognition. This research program takes that parallel seriously.

## Papers

- **The Stone's Shadow** (v0.1) — On the relationship between optimization and understanding
- **Digital Philosopher's Stone** (v0.1) — Phase transitions in capability emergence
- **Economic Transmutation** (v0.1) — Why AI labor displacement has no historical precedent

## Models & Analysis

- `models/Probabilistic_Models_Analysis.docx` — Formal probabilistic framework
- `models/magnum_opus.html` — Interactive visualization of the transmutation thesis
- `models/the_great_transmutation.html` — Economic transformation model
